Instagram "Suspicious Login Attempt" — Was It You? What to Do Right Now

By Md Alamin Islam · 21/10/2026

Instagram sometimes sends a message saying "We noticed a suspicious login attempt" or "Was this you?". It can be a real security warning, or it can be a fake message from a scammer. The safe move is to check inside the official app, not through a link in a message. Wording and menus may change over time.

First, check if the message is real

  • Open the Instagram app yourself. Do not tap links inside emails, DMs or SMS.
  • Go to Settings > Accounts Center > Password and security > Emails from Instagram. You can see recent official emails there.
  • Look at the sender. A real email comes from an official Instagram domain, but even then it is safer to verify inside the app.

If it was you

Tap "This was me" and continue. This is common when you use a new phone, VPN or different Wi-Fi.

If it was not you

1. Tap "This wasn't me" and follow the steps to secure your account. 2. Change your password. Use a long, unique one that you do not use anywhere else. 3. Go to Password and security > Where you're logged in and log out of devices you do not know. 4. Turn on Two-Factor Authentication, preferably with an authentication app rather than SMS. 5. Check your email address and phone number. If they were changed, change them back. 6. Remove apps you do not recognize in Settings > Website permissions or Apps and websites.

Signs that the message is a scam

  • It asks for your password or code outside the Instagram app.
  • It says your account will be deleted in 24 hours.
  • It has spelling mistakes or an odd sender address.
  • It asks you to pay to "verify" your account.

If you cannot log in anymore

Use "Forgot password?" on the login screen and the email or phone you used when you created the account. If that does not work, use the Instagram Help Center and follow the official account recovery steps.

Keep your account safe in the future

  • Use a strong, unique password.
  • Turn on Two-Factor Authentication.
  • Never share your codes with anyone.
  • Log out from shared devices.
