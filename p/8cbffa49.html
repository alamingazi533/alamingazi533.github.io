Facebook Monetization Policies Appeal — How to Request a Review and What to Check First

By Md Alamin Islam · 13/10/2026

If Facebook limits or removes monetization on your page or profile, you may see messages such as "Your ability to earn is limited" or a notice that content does not meet monetization policies. This can be stressful, especially if you earn income from your content. This guide explains the general process of checking the reason and requesting a review. Programs, menu names and eligibility rules change often, so always compare with Facebook's official help pages.

Step 1: Find the exact notice

Open your Professional Dashboard or Support Inbox and look for the message about monetization. Note what it says: which page or profile, which policy, and whether there is a review or appeal button.

Step 2: Understand the likely reason

  • Your content may be reposted, copied or reused from others.
  • Videos may use music, clips or images you do not own.
  • Content may be misleading, spammy or low in original value.
  • There may be a sensitive-topic or community standards issue.
  • Account or payout details may be incomplete or incorrect.

Step 3: Review your recent content

Look through the last posts and videos. Remove or edit content you cannot prove is yours or that clearly breaks the rules. Replace it with original material.

Step 4: Request a review

If a "Request review" or "Appeal" button is shown, use it and answer honestly. Explain briefly that your content is original and describe what you changed. Submit only one request for each case unless asked for more.

Step 5: Wait and monitor

Reviews can take from a few days to a few weeks. Keep posting normally, but follow the rules carefully while you wait.

Step 6: Check payout and tax details

Open your payout settings and make sure your name, tax information and bank or payment method are correct and match your official details.

How to protect monetization long term

  • Create original videos, photos and text.
  • Use licensed or platform-provided music.
  • Avoid clickbait and misleading headlines.
  • Do not buy followers, views or engagement.
  • Read the monetization policies regularly.

Important

No one can guarantee that a review will be approved. Never pay anyone who claims to have a secret way to reinstate monetization, and never share your password. We are an independent help site and not affiliated with Meta.
