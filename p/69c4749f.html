WhatsApp Chat Backup and Restore — How It Works, Why Backup Fails and How to Move Chats to a New Phone

Losing years of WhatsApp chats when you change phones is painful, and most of the time it happens because the backup was never set up properly. This guide explains how WhatsApp backup works, how to restore chats on a new phone, and what to check when the backup keeps failing. Menu names change between versions, so if your screen looks slightly different, look for the closest option.

How WhatsApp backup works

WhatsApp keeps your chats on your phone. A backup is a copy saved to your Google Drive account on Android or your iCloud account on iPhone. When you install WhatsApp on a new phone and verify the same phone number, it offers to restore that copy. Without a backup, your old messages cannot be brought back by WhatsApp itself.

How to turn on a backup

  • Open WhatsApp and go to Settings.
  • Tap Chats, then Chat backup.
  • Choose how often to back up: daily, weekly or monthly.
  • On Android, pick the Google account that will hold the backup. On iPhone, make sure iCloud Drive is switched on.
  • Tap Back up to create the first copy right now.

You can also choose whether to include videos. Videos make the backup much larger, so many people leave them out.

End-to-end encrypted backup

WhatsApp also offers an optional end-to-end encrypted backup. When you switch it on, you protect the backup with a password or a long key. Remember this password carefully, because WhatsApp cannot recover it for you. If you forget it, the backup becomes unusable.

How to restore chats on a new phone

  • Install WhatsApp on the new phone and open it.
  • Verify the same phone number you used before.
  • When asked, tap Restore, and wait until it finishes.
  • Tap Next and let your chats load.

To restore from Google Drive, you must sign in to the same Google account on the new Android phone. To restore from iCloud, you must use the same Apple ID. A backup from Android cannot normally be restored on an iPhone, and the reverse is also true, so check the current WhatsApp help pages if you are changing platform.

Why backup fails

  • Your Google Drive or iCloud storage is full. Free up space or buy more storage.
  • You are on weak mobile data, and the backup is set to Wi-Fi only.
  • Google Play services are out of date, or you are signed out of your Google account.
  • The phone battery is low or the phone is not charging.
  • The date and time on the phone are wrong.

Fix the simple causes first, then run the backup again.

Safety warnings

Never share your six-digit verification code or your backup password with anyone. Scammers sometimes claim they can "restore your chats" or "recover your account" if you send them a code. That is a trick to take over your WhatsApp. Real restoration happens only inside the WhatsApp app on your own phone.

Quick checklist before changing phones

  • Make a fresh backup and wait until it says it is finished.
  • Keep your old phone and SIM until the new phone has restored everything.
  • Check that you remember your Google or Apple login.
  • Note down your encrypted backup password, if you use one.

Frequently asked questions

Question: Can I restore my chats without a backup? Answer: Not through WhatsApp itself. If you have no backup, a local backup file on the old phone may exist, but this is limited, so make a proper backup before you change phones.

Question: Will restoring delete my new messages? Answer: Restoring brings back older chats from the backup. Messages you received after the backup was taken may be missing, so back up again before you switch.

Question: How often should I back up? Answer: Daily is safest for active users. If you use WhatsApp for business, a daily backup is worth the extra storage.
