WhatsApp "Delete for Everyone" Explained — Time Limit, What the Other Person Sees and Why It Sometimes Fails

You sent a message to the wrong person, or you wrote something you did not mean to. The first thing most people want to know is whether WhatsApp can pull the message back. It can, using Delete for Everyone, but only under certain conditions. This guide explains how it works, how long you have, and the limits you should know about. WhatsApp updates its features from time to time, so check the app if anything differs.

Delete for me and Delete for Everyone

When you long-press a message and tap Delete, WhatsApp offers two choices. Delete for me removes the message only from your own chat. The other person still sees it. Delete for Everyone removes it from your chat and from the chats of the people who received it, and everyone in the chat sees a note saying the message was deleted.

How much time do you have

Delete for Everyone works only for a limited time after sending. At the time of writing, this is roughly two days and twelve hours, but WhatsApp has changed the limit before and can change it again. If you notice a mistake, act quickly. After the limit passes, only Delete for me remains.

How to delete a message for everyone

  • Open the chat and long-press the message you sent.
  • You can select several messages at once.
  • Tap the bin icon, or Delete.
  • Choose Delete for everyone.

What the other person may still see

  • A note such as "This message was deleted" stays in the chat. The people in the chat know that something was removed, though not what it said.
  • If they already read it, you cannot make them forget it, and they may have taken a screenshot.
  • Notifications may show the message text on the other person's lock screen for a short time, and some phones keep notification history.
  • Photos and videos may already be saved in the recipient's gallery if automatic download was on.
  • If the other person uses a very old version of WhatsApp, the deletion may not work properly.

Because of these limits, Delete for Everyone should be treated as damage reduction, not a guarantee.

Why the option does not appear

  • The time limit has already passed.
  • You are trying to delete a message sent by someone else. You can only delete for everyone the messages you sent.
  • The app is out of date. Update WhatsApp and try again.
  • The message was not delivered yet because the phone is offline. Connect to the internet first.

If you shared something sensitive

If you sent a password, a one-time code, bank details or an identity document, do not rely on deleting the message. Change the password, report the lost code to the service, and contact your bank if needed. Deleting the chat message does not undo the exposure.

Group chats

In groups, the same rule applies. Group admins may also have options to remove messages of other members in some versions, but that depends on the group settings and app version, so check what your app shows.

Good habits

  • Read twice before you send, especially on a phone where the Send button sits next to other buttons.
  • Use disappearing messages for chats where you do not want long records.
  • Do not send private photos or documents unless you trust the person and the situation.

Frequently asked questions

Question: If I delete for everyone, will the other person get a notification? Answer: They see that a message was deleted in the chat. They are not told what it said, but they may have already seen it in a notification.

Question: Can I delete a voice message or photo for everyone? Answer: Yes, in the same way as a text message. A copy may remain in the other person's gallery if it was auto-saved.

Question: Can I undo a deleted message? Answer: WhatsApp lets you undo a Delete for me for a few seconds right after you do it. A Delete for everyone cannot be undone, so you would need to send the message again.
