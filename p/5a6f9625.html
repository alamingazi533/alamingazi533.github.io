Instagram "Sorry, There Was a Problem With Your Request" — What It Means and How to Fix It

By Md Alamin Islam · 21/10/2026

You try to log in, follow someone, or post on Instagram and see "Sorry, there was a problem with your request" or "Try again later". The message is short and does not explain much, which is why it frustrates so many people. In most cases it is a temporary block, a weak connection, or an app glitch, not a permanent ban. Menu names and screens may change over time, so use this as a general guide.

Common reasons

  • A weak or unstable internet connection.
  • Too many login attempts or actions in a short time.
  • An outdated Instagram app.
  • A VPN or proxy that Instagram does not trust.
  • Corrupted app cache.
  • A temporary issue on Instagram's own servers.
  • A new device or location that looks unusual.

What to try, in order

1. Close the app fully, wait a few minutes, and open it again. 2. Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data. Turn off any VPN. 3. Update Instagram from the Play Store or App Store. 4. Clear the app cache from your phone settings. This does not delete your account. 5. Restart your phone. 6. Try logging in from the Instagram website in your browser. 7. If you are not logged in, use "Forgot password?" only once and follow the steps. 8. If you are logged in but one action fails, stop that action for several hours and try again later.

Do not do this

  • Do not tap the same button again and again. It can extend the block.
  • Do not use third-party apps that promise to "fix" Instagram or add followers.
  • Do not give your password or code to anyone claiming to help.

When to wait

If the problem started suddenly and nothing seems wrong with your phone or internet, it may be on Instagram's side. Waiting a few hours or a day often solves it. If friends report the same issue, it is likely a general outage.

If it never goes away

Open Instagram Help Center and follow the official account support steps. Keep your login email or phone number ready so you can verify yourself.

Final note: we are not affiliated with Instagram or Meta. Patience, a stable connection and a clean app usually fix this message.
