How to Turn On Two-Factor Authentication on Instagram — Step-by-Step Setup and Backup Codes

By Md Alamin Islam · 21/10/2026

A strong password is not enough on its own. If someone learns your password, Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) adds one more step that they usually cannot pass. This guide shows how to turn on 2FA on Instagram and keep a backup so you do not lock yourself out. Menu names and screens may change over time.

Why 2FA matters

  • It protects your account even if your password leaks.
  • It stops many hijacking attempts.
  • It alerts you when someone tries to log in from a new device.

Setting it up

1. Open Instagram and go to your profile. Tap the menu, then Accounts Center. 2. Choose Password and security, then Two-factor authentication. 3. Select your Instagram account. 4. Pick a method:

  • Authentication app (recommended): such as Google Authenticator. It generates codes on your phone.
  • Text message (SMS): a code is sent to your phone number.
  • WhatsApp: some regions offer codes through WhatsApp.

5. Follow the on-screen steps. For an authentication app, scan the code or enter the key, then type the 6-digit code to confirm. 6. Save your backup codes if Instagram shows them. Take a screenshot or write them down in a safe place.

Why an authentication app is better

SMS codes can fail if your SIM is lost or the network is weak, and in some cases they can be intercepted. An authentication app works without the network and is generally safer.

Keep your account from locking you out

  • Save backup codes in a private place, not on a public cloud note.
  • Keep your recovery email and phone number up to date.
  • If you change your phone, move your authenticator app first, before wiping the old phone.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Never share a login code with anyone, even someone who claims to be Instagram support.
  • Do not approve a login request you did not start.
  • Do not turn off 2FA just because it feels inconvenient.

Final note: we are not affiliated with Instagram or Meta. Two minutes of setup now can save you from losing your account later.
