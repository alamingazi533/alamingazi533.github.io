Facebook Hidden Messages — How to Find Message Requests and Filtered Messages You Missed

By Md Alamin Islam · 21/10/2026

Many people miss important Facebook messages because they land in a hidden folder. A customer, a friend you are not connected with, or an old contact may have messaged you, but you never saw a notification. Facebook puts some messages in Message Requests or Filtered Messages instead of your main inbox. Menu names change over time, so look for the closest option.

Why messages go to a hidden folder

  • The sender is not your Facebook friend.
  • Facebook thinks the message may be spam or unwanted.
  • Your privacy settings send messages from non-friends to Message Requests.
  • The sender has a new or low-trust account.

How to find them in the Facebook app

1. Open Messenger from the Facebook app or the Messenger app. 2. Tap your profile picture or the menu icon. 3. Look for "Message requests" (or "Chat requests"). 4. Check the tabs inside, such as "You may know" and "Spam". 5. Open any message to read it. You can Accept, Delete or Ignore.

How to find them on a computer

1. Go to facebook.com and open Messenger. 2. Click the three-dot menu in the chat list. 3. Choose "Message requests" and then "Spam" or "Filtered".

Page messages

If you run a Page, open Meta Business Suite (or the Pages Inbox) and check the "Spam" or "Other" folder in the Inbox as well.

Good habits

  • Check Message Requests once a day if you take orders or work through messages.
  • Turn on notifications for Messenger in your phone settings.
  • Reply quickly to genuine customers.

Stay safe

  • Do not click links from unknown people in message requests.
  • Do not share your password, code or payment details.
  • If a request looks like a scam, delete it and report the account.

Final note: we are not affiliated with Meta. A short daily check of hidden folders can save you from missing real messages.
