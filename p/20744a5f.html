WhatsApp "Couldn't Link Device" — Why Linking WhatsApp Web or a Second Phone Fails and How to Fix It

WhatsApp lets you use your account on WhatsApp Web, the desktop app, or a companion device. Sometimes the QR code scan fails, the screen says "Couldn't link device," or the linked device logs out repeatedly. This guide explains the common causes and gives a practical order of fixes. WhatsApp changes its menus and limits from time to time, so exact names on your phone may differ slightly.

What "linked devices" means

A linked device is another screen, such as a computer or tablet, that connects to your main WhatsApp account without needing your phone to stay online all the time. You usually link a device by scanning a QR code from your phone. Because linking gives another device access to your chats, WhatsApp also uses security checks that can fail for harmless reasons.

Common reasons linking fails

  • Weak or unstable internet on the phone or the computer.
  • The WhatsApp app on your phone is outdated.
  • The phone's date and time are wrong.
  • You already reached the maximum number of linked devices.
  • The QR code expired before you scanned it.
  • A VPN, proxy or strict firewall is blocking the connection.
  • A temporary WhatsApp server problem.
  • Your account has a temporary restriction.

Fixes to try, in order

Step 1: Check the internet on both devices. Switch from Wi-Fi to mobile data, or the other way round, then try again. Step 2: Update WhatsApp on your phone from the official app store. Step 3: Set the phone's date and time to automatic. Step 4: On the phone, open Settings, then Linked devices. Remove any old or unknown devices to free a slot. Step 5: Refresh the WhatsApp Web page to get a fresh QR code, then scan it quickly with good lighting and a clean camera lens. Step 6: Turn off any VPN on the phone and the computer. Step 7: Restart the phone and the browser, or use a different browser. Step 8: Clear the browser cache or try a private window. Step 9: If nothing works, wait some hours and try again.

If the device keeps logging out

Linked devices can disconnect if your phone has not been online for a long period. Keep your main phone connected to the internet at least occasionally. Also check whether battery optimization is killing WhatsApp in the background. On some phones you need to allow background activity for the app.

Security: check what is linked

Open Settings, then Linked devices. If you see a device you do not recognize, tap it and log out. An unknown linked device is one way a stranger can read your chats. After removing it, turn on two-step verification and change any other passwords that may be connected.

Never link a device for someone else

Scammers sometimes ask you to scan a QR code "to verify your account" or "to receive a prize." Scanning gives them access. Only scan a code from a WhatsApp screen you opened yourself.

When to contact support

If linking still fails after the steps above, use the in-app help (Settings, then Help) to report the problem. Include your phone model, WhatsApp version and the exact message you see.

Frequently asked questions

Can I link WhatsApp to a second phone? Many accounts can use a companion phone feature, but availability depends on your app version, so check what your app offers.

Do my chats stay safe on linked devices? Messages stay end-to-end encrypted, but anyone with access to the linked device can read them.
