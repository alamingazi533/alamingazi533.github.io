Monetization Policy Appeal: How to Request a Review on Facebook and YouTube

By Md Alamin Islam · 22/10/2026

If your monetization was turned off or rejected, you may be able to ask for a review. An appeal does not guarantee approval, but a clear and honest one gives you the best chance. Menu names and screens may change over time.

First, find out why

  • Open your Monetization status or Policy issues page. On Facebook, check Page Quality or Monetization Eligibility in Meta Business Suite. On YouTube, open YouTube Studio and look under Earn or the Content tab.
  • Read the exact reason shown. Common reasons are reused content, copyright, misleading content, or not meeting the eligibility rules.

How to appeal

1. Fix the problem first. Remove or replace content that breaks the rules. 2. Wait until the review option shows. Do not send repeated requests. 3. Click the Request review or Appeal option. 4. Write a short, polite and honest explanation of what you changed. 5. Submit once and wait. Reviews can take several days.

Tips

  • Use your own original photos, videos and text.
  • Do not claim things that are not true.
  • Do not buy followers or views.
  • Keep checking your email and notifications for the result.

If the appeal is rejected, improve your content for some weeks and apply again when the option appears. You can send us your case through the form if you need guidance.
