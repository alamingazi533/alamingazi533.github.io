WhatsApp Message Shows One Grey Tick — Why It Isn't Delivered and What to Do

By Md Alamin Islam · 21/10/2026

You send a WhatsApp message and it shows only one grey tick. That means the message has left your phone, but it has not reached the other person's phone yet. This is very common and often not serious. This guide explains the tick symbols and the reasons behind a single tick. Screens and wording may change over time.

What the ticks mean

  • One grey tick: sent from your phone, not yet delivered.
  • Two grey ticks: delivered to the other person's phone.
  • Two blue ticks: the other person has read it, if read receipts are on.
  • A clock icon: the message is still waiting to send because your phone has a problem.

Why you may see only one tick

  • The other person's phone is off, in airplane mode, or has no internet.
  • The other person blocked you.
  • The other person has deleted WhatsApp or changed their number.
  • Their phone is saving data in a way that delays messages.
  • Your own internet is weak, so the delivery is slow.
  • There is a temporary problem on WhatsApp's servers.

How to check, step by step

1. Check your own internet. Open a website to confirm you are online. 2. Wait for a few minutes. Many single ticks turn into two ticks soon. 3. Ask a mutual friend to see if the person is active. 4. Look at the person's profile. If you can no longer see their photo or "Last seen", it may be privacy settings or a block, but this is only a hint, not proof. 5. Try calling them on WhatsApp. If it never rings, they may have you blocked or have no connection. 6. Restart your phone and try again.

Do not do this

  • Do not send the same message many times. It looks like spam and can add to the problem.
  • Do not assume a single tick means a block. It has many other causes.
  • Do not install apps that claim to show if someone blocked you. They are not reliable and can steal your data.

If the message never delivers

Try a normal SMS or a phone call. If it is important, contact the person another way.

Final note: we are not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta. A single tick is usually a delay, not a ban, so stay calm and check your connection first.
