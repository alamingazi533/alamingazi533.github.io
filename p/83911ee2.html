Facebook "Your Account Has Been Locked" — What It Means and How to Unlock It

By Md Alamin Islam · 21/10/2026

You open Facebook and see "Your account has been locked" or "We've locked your account to protect it". It looks scary, but a lock is usually a temporary safety step, not a permanent ban. Facebook does this when it sees something unusual, such as a login from a new place, many fast actions, or signs that someone else may have your password. Menu names and screens may change over time, so look for the closest option in your app.

Why Facebook locks an account

  • A login from a new device, city or country.
  • Many friend requests, messages or posts in a short time.
  • Signs that your password may have been leaked or guessed.
  • Reports from other people about your activity.
  • A link or app you used that looks unsafe.

What to do, step by step

1. Tap "Get started" (or "Continue") on the lock screen. Do not close the page. 2. Confirm your identity in the way Facebook asks. This can be a code sent to your email or phone, a photo of a government ID, or a video selfie. 3. Take the photo in good light, with your full face visible and without filters. 4. If you upload an ID, use a clear photo where the name, photo and date of birth are readable. The name should match your Facebook profile. 5. Wait for the review. It can take a few hours, and sometimes a few days. 6. When the account opens, change your password at once and turn on Two-Factor Authentication. 7. Check Settings > Accounts Center > Password and security > Where you're logged in, and log out of every device you do not know.

Do not do this

  • Do not create many new accounts to "test" the lock. It can make things worse.
  • Do not send your ID or password to anyone who says they can unlock the account for money. Real unlocking happens only inside Facebook's own screens.
  • Do not keep pressing "Submit" again and again. One clear submission is enough.

If the lock does not go away

If you cannot pass the identity check, or you see no option to continue, wait a day and try again from a device you normally use, on your usual Wi-Fi. If the account is still stuck, use the official Help Center and report the problem from the lock screen if that option appears.

After you get back in

Review recent posts and messages, remove apps you do not recognize, and update your recovery email and phone number. A small cleanup now helps you avoid the same lock later.

Final note: we are not affiliated with Meta, and the result depends on Facebook's own checks. Stay calm, follow only the official steps, and keep your details accurate.
