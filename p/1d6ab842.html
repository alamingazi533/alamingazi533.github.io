Facebook Confirmation Code Not Arriving? 9 Fixes for Login and Password Reset Codes

By Md Alamin Islam · 13/10/2026

You tap "Send code" on Facebook, but the SMS or email never comes. This happens when you log in, reset your password or confirm a new device. Before you panic, work through the checklist below. In many cases the problem is a simple setting or a delay, not a problem with your account. Menu names may change over time.

Why the code may not arrive

  • The phone number or email on the account is old or no longer yours.
  • Your mobile carrier delays or blocks short-code SMS messages.
  • The email went to Spam, Promotions or another folder.
  • Your phone has no signal, is full, or blocks unknown senders.
  • You requested too many codes in a short time, so Facebook paused sending.
  • The account has a different contact point than the one you are checking.

Fixes to try, in order

1. Wait two to five minutes. Codes can be delayed. 2. Check your Spam, Junk, Promotions and Social email folders. 3. Make sure you have network signal and enough phone storage. 4. Restart your phone and try once more. 5. Turn off call or message blocking apps for a short time. 6. Choose a different delivery method if offered, such as email instead of SMS, or the other way around. 7. Try from a device and place you normally use. Facebook trusts familiar devices more. 8. Use the "Try another way" option and see which options appear. 9. If you still have the app logged in on another device, go to Accounts Center and check your contact details there.

Do not do this

  • Do not tap "Send code" many times in a row. Too many requests can pause sending for hours.
  • Do not give your code to anyone, even if they claim to be from Facebook support.
  • Do not click links in messages that ask you to "verify your account" outside the official app or facebook.com.

If you no longer have access to the phone number or email

Use the official "Forgot password" or "No longer have access to these?" option on the login page, and follow the identity check steps shown. Use only official Facebook pages for this. If you cannot recover the account, creating a new one is possible, but remember it is a different account.

Final note: we are not affiliated with Meta, and recovery results depend on Facebook's own checks. Stay patient, avoid repeated attempts, and keep your contact details updated after you get back in.
