Facebook "Your Ability to Earn Is Limited" — What It Means and What to Check

By Md Alamin Islam · 21/10/2026

If you open Professional Dashboard or Monetization and see "Your ability to earn is limited" or "Monetization: Limited", your page or profile can no longer earn money from some features, at least for now. This does not always mean your account is gone. It means Facebook has put a limit on earning because of something it found. Menu names and wording may change, so use this as a general guide.

What this status usually points to

  • Content that looks copied, reused or not original.
  • A Community Standards or Monetization Policy problem on the page.
  • Content that may not be suitable for ads.
  • Missing or unclear payout details or tax information.
  • A change in your page ownership, name or country.

Where to look

1. Open the page or profile, then go to Professional Dashboard > Monetization. 2. Check the "Status" or "Issues" section and read the message carefully. 3. Open any listed policy and read the exact rule you are said to have broken. 4. Look at your recent posts and reels. Check for reused videos, watermarks from other apps, or clips you did not make. 5. Open Settings and Account Quality (or Account Status) to see if there are other restrictions.

What you can do

  • Remove or edit content that does not follow the rules, especially copied videos and reposts.
  • Post original content: your own videos, photos and writing.
  • Make sure payout and tax details are complete and correct.
  • If you think the decision is wrong, use "Request review" (or "Appeal") where it appears. Do it once, and be honest.
  • Wait for the review result. It can take several days.

Do not do this

  • Do not delete the page in a panic. It can remove any chance of review.
  • Do not buy "monetization unlock" services. They cannot override Facebook.
  • Do not keep posting the same kind of content that caused the limit.

How long it lasts

Some limits are lifted after a review or after the problem is fixed. Others stay for a while. We cannot promise a date, and Facebook decides each case.
